Workshop Schedule

Five days of musical exploration · 02–06 November 2026

Draft programme framework only. Replace the session titles and timings with the approved timetable before public circulation.
02NOV

Day 1 · सुरों से संवाद

Orientation, musical foundations, shared expectations and collaborative singing.

03NOV

Day 2 · Bansuri: Breath, Notes & Expression

Introduction to Bansuri, posture, breath control, fingering and guided practice.

04NOV

Day 3 · राग, लय और सृजन

Exploring Raag, rhythm, listening, improvisation and creative classroom connections.

05NOV

Day 4 · Music for Every Child

Music pedagogy, inclusive participation, assembly practices and studio recording.

06NOV

Day 5 · प्रस्तुति और आत्मचिंतन

Group presentations, sharing of best practices, reflection and valedictory.
